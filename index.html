Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

How many matches will I play?
Every participant plays multiple matches. ArenaX uses a structured format that ensures enough performance data for a meaningful assessment — not a single-match elimination format.
Is this a regular knockout tournament?
No. ArenaX combines match play with performance evaluation. The goal is skill assessment and official ranking, not elimination. Everyone gets ranked regardless of win/loss record.
Does everyone get a certificate?
Yes — every participant receives a performance-based certificate containing their official rank, skill category, and performance band. This is NOT a participation certificate. It contains real, assessed data.
Is ArenaX for beginners?
ArenaX is designed for developing and competitive players who want structured feedback on their game. Participants are matched with others of similar level and age, so assessments are meaningful.
How does payment work?
Register via the form to hold your slot — registration itself is free. A payment link will be sent to your WhatsApp number within 24 hours. Your slot is only confirmed upon payment. No on-spot registrations.
Is Mixed Doubles available?
Based on registrations, ArenaX may introduce additional pairing formats including Mixed Doubles. Confirmed category details will be shared before the event.
