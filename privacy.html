Who we are

This website is operated by ArenaX Digital Solutions, a Udyam-registered MSME based in Yamunanagar, Haryana, India. AdhyX is a product of ArenaX Digital Solutions.

You can contact us about this policy or your personal data at contactarenax@gmail.com.

What this website collects

  • No cookies. This website does not set any cookies.
  • No analytics. This website does not use analytics, tracking pixels or advertising tools.
  • No third-party resources on the pages. All fonts, images and scripts are served from this website itself. Your browser does not contact any other service when you open these pages. It contacts Google only when you send one of the enquiry forms (see below).
  • Enquiry forms. When you send an enquiry form, we collect what you type into it, described in the next section.

The enquiry forms

The forms on this website (Request a demo lecture, Scope a pilot, First-school list and Contact) ask for your name, role, institution, type of institution, email address and, on some forms, a preferred month and a message. They do not ask for a phone number.

When you press the button, your enquiry is sent to a Google Apps Script service run by ArenaX Digital Solutions in its Google account. That service:

  • saves your enquiry as one row in a Google Sheet held in that account, together with the date and time and the page you sent it from; and
  • emails a copy of your enquiry to contactarenax@gmail.com, so we can reply to you.

Google stores this data for us as part of its Google Workspace services, and may process it on servers outside India, as set out in Google's own privacy policy. We use your enquiry only to reply to you and to follow up on what you asked about. We do not sell it, share it for advertising or add you to a mailing list. We keep it for no longer than we need to for that purpose, and we delete it if you ask us to.

If the form cannot reach that service, it opens your own email app with a message addressed to contactarenax@gmail.com and your details filled in. Nothing is sent in that case until you choose to send that email yourself.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your email and the details in it only to reply to you. We keep it for no longer than we need to for that purpose, and we delete it if you ask us to.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data of yours we hold and how we use it (access);
  • correct or update it (correction);
  • erase it (erasure);
  • address a complaint about how we have handled it (grievance).

To do any of these, write to contactarenax@gmail.com.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operation of its service, as set out in GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not receive or use those logs.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date.
