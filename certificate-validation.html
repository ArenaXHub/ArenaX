Where to find the Certificate ID

  • The Certificate ID is printed at the bottom-right of every ArenaX Performance Certificate.
  • It's also included in the WhatsApp message you receive with your certificate delivery.
  • Format: AX-YYYY-NNN — e.g. AX-2026-001
  • Certificate IDs are case-insensitive. You can enter in any case — the system handles it.
