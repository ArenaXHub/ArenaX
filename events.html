How Performance Ranking Is Determined

Participants are evaluated through structured match-based and skill-based assessment conducted by ArenaX-appointed evaluators.

  • Technical shot execution
  • Footwork and movement efficiency
  • Match application and decision-making
  • Consistency during evaluation matches

Performance Bands Explained

Performance Bands indicate a participant’s standing relative to the total evaluated pool in the event.

Top 10% – Among the highest-performing players
Top 25% – Strong competitive performance within the cohort

Rankings represent relative performance within the event cohort and are not based on participation alone.
