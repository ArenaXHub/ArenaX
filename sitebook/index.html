Ek worker, chaar bade button

☀️ 8 ghante, ☀️🌙 12 ghante OT, 🌓 4 ghante, ❌ nahi aaya. Hours map to days the way the register does: 8 h = 1 day, 12 h = 1.5, 4 h = half. The ± stepper handles 9, 10, 11 h.

Kharchi: notes tap karo

Tap 💵 on a worker, then tap ₹100, ₹200, ₹500 notes. The advance is logged against the worker and the week, same as the POCKET column.

The same week grid as your Excel, from your Excel. Today's column fills as you tap.
