ArenaX Student Internship & Volunteer Program

A student-led initiative building the future of data-driven sports events.

ArenaX is inviting motivated students to join the founding team for its pilot project, the ArenaX Badminton Skill Challenge.

Students will contribute to designing and executing structured sports events, including participant management, performance tracking, and skill-based certification.

Roles Available

  • Event Operations
  • Event Data & Analytics
  • Digital Promotion
  • Brand Partnerships & Sponsorship
  • Participation & Campus Outreach

What Students Gain

  • Real-world event execution experience
  • Exposure to data-driven event systems
  • Performance-based internship certificates
  • LinkedIn recommendations
  • Letter of appreciation
  • Recognition as ArenaX Founding Team Members
